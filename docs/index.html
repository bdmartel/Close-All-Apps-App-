❓ How does this work?

iOS does not allow any app to close other apps directly — it is a core security feature of the operating system. Instead, this app guides you through the fastest methods to close all your running apps manually.

⚡ Quick Methods

  • App Switcher Swipe — The standard way. Swipe up on each app card to close it.
  • Multi-finger Swipe — Use 2-3 fingers to swipe up multiple cards at once.
  • AssistiveTouch — Set up a custom gesture to speed things up.

💡 Tips

  • Battery
    iOS manages background apps efficiently. Closing apps is rarely needed to save battery — but it can help if an app is misbehaving.
  • Memory
    The system automatically frees memory when needed. Force-closing is most useful when an app is frozen or glitching.
  • Privacy
    Closing apps removes them from the App Switcher, which can help keep your activity private when handing your phone to someone.

How to Close All Apps

Recommended App Switcher

  1. 1Open the App Switcher. On iPhones with Face ID, swipe up from the bottom and pause in the center. On iPhones with a Home button, double-press the Home button.
  2. 2Swipe apps away. Swipe each app card upward to close it. You can use two or three fingers to swipe multiple apps at the same time.
  3. 3Repeat until all app cards are gone. That is it — all apps are closed.

Alternative Multi-finger Technique

  1. 1Open the App Switcher as described above.
  2. 2Place two or three fingers on separate app cards at the same time.
  3. 3Swipe all fingers upward simultaneously to close multiple apps in one gesture.
  4. 4Repeat until all apps are dismissed.

Accessibility AssistiveTouch

  1. 1Go to Settings > Accessibility > Touch > AssistiveTouch and turn it on.
  2. 2Tap "Create New Gesture" and record a three-finger upward swipe.
  3. 3Save the gesture with a name like "Close Apps".
  4. 4Open the App Switcher, tap the AssistiveTouch button, choose Custom, and select your saved gesture to close multiple apps at once.
